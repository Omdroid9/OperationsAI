SkyOS

From signal to compliance

I built a tool that helps a compliance team go from “who should we look at?” all the way to “the work is done and we’re watching renewals.”

How I approached the project skyos-two.vercel.app

Track choice

I started from Remove Friction, then pulled in the other tracks where they helped.

The brief has four directions: Grow, Automate, Experience, and Remove Friction. Mixing was allowed when it still solved one clear problem. That fit what I wanted to build.

PRIMARY

Remove Friction

One workspace instead of sheets, calls, and scattered tools.

ALSO

Grow

Find carriers, start outbound qualify calls from CRM, move the lead.

ALSO

Automate

Outbound calling from CRM, lookups, document drafts, renewals.

ALSO

Experience

Consultation on the site through to a clear case.

One story. Pieces from all four tracks. Not four separate apps. 02

The problem I targeted

The work lives in too many places.

  • Finding good leads is still mostly manual The public data is out there. “Why this carrier?” is stuck in a spreadsheet or someone’s memory.
  • Qualifying means leaving the CRM to dial Outbound calls, notes, and stage should stay on the same opportunity, not in a separate phone app.
  • After you win the deal, everything scatters again Documents, renewals, and checklists become another pile of tools.
I wasn’t trying to build “more AI.” I wanted one clear path from signal to finished work. 03

Who it’s for

I designed it for the team doing the work, and for carriers who come in through the website.

THE TEAM

Staff in the workspace

  • See who needs attention today
  • Qualify from the CRM with an outbound call
  • Turn a win into a case with documents
  • Watch rules that might affect customers

CARRIERS

On the public site

  • Book a free consultation
  • Get a practical next step, not promises we can’t keep
  • Later, documents and renewals sit in one case
Website is for intake. The app is where staff do the daily loop. 04

The idea

I kept everything on one loop.

Signal→ Opportunity→ Review→ Qualify→ CRM→ Convert→ Case→ Monitor

Public data can suggest someone might need help. Review happens before outreach. Facts we know and guesses we make stay separate.

Built for day-to-day ops work, with calling baked into the CRM flow. 06

How I’m careful with claims

Public FMCSA data doesn’t mean they want to buy, and it doesn’t prove they’re out of compliance.

I say things like

  • Possible need
  • Worth a look
  • Recommend a review
  • Suggested next step

I don’t say

  • They definitely need us
  • They’re noncompliant (unless verified)
  • Guaranteed approval or sale
Clear language matters when you’re talking about compliance. 07

What I learned

A few things that stuck

I learned

  • Showing where a fact came from helps people use the product
  • Demo and Live need a clear split so the walkthrough doesn’t mix with real data
  • Confirm after extract keeps document review simple
  • Outbound calling works best when it starts from the CRM record

I’d tighten next

  • Cleaner save and delete of live data
  • Stronger access rules for staff
  • Real file storage with a clear history
  • Better empty states when Live has no data yet
Try. Learn. Explain. That matched what the brief asked for. 17

Future scope

GROW

Same intake pipe as Meta: paid or organic forms become leads with clear source labels.

FULFILL

Billing handoff

When a case is Won, create the draft invoice or sync the customer into QuickBooks.

Still the same loop. More ways for work to enter, and fewer handoffs out. 18
